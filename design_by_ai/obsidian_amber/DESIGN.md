## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for the professional Ricoh GR enthusiast, mirroring the camera’s own philosophy: "The Tool for the Task." It prioritizes speed, precision, and a distraction-free environment. 

The visual style is **macOS-inspired Minimalism with Glassmorphic depth**. It leverages high-transparency layers and background blurs to maintain context while browsing image libraries. The emotional response is one of premium craftsmanship—utilitarian yet sophisticated—evoking the tactile feel of magnesium alloy and high-quality optics.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in a deep **Obsidian (#1A1A1A)**, providing a pitch-black canvas that allows photography to take center stage. 

- **Primary (Signature Orange):** Used exclusively for high-priority actions like the shutter release, active states, and critical highlights.
- **Surface & Chrome:** Utilizes Charcoal and Slate tones for subtle differentiation between panels.
- **Vibrancy:** The system relies on "Desktop-class" translucency, where the background colors of the UI are generated by blurring the underlying content or the system wallpaper.

## Typography

The typography uses **Inter** (as the closest web-accessible relative to SF Pro) to maintain a clean, systematic look. For technical data—such as EXIF information (ISO, Aperture, Shutter Speed)—**JetBrains Mono** is employed to give a precise, horological feel.

All type should be rendered with `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`. Hierarchy is established through weight and color (using 100%, 70%, and 45% white opacity) rather than excessive size shifts.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses a **layered glass approach** to depth:

1.  **Level 0 (Background):** Pure #1A1A1A or a blurred representation of the user's wallpaper.
2.  **Level 1 (Sidebars/Toolbars):** 70% opacity #2C2C2C with a 30px backdrop-blur. A 0.5px solid white border at 10% opacity creates a "sharp" edge.
3.  **Level 2 (Cards/Modals):** 85% opacity #333333 with a subtle 16px drop shadow (0px 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)).
4.  **Level 3 (Popovers/Tooltips):** Solid #404040 with a more aggressive shadow to indicate immediate priority.

Avoid heavy drop shadows; depth is primarily communicated via the contrast between blurred background layers and crisp foreground borders.

## Shapes

Following macOS standards, the design system uses **Soft (Level 1)** roundedness. 

- **Standard Elements:** 6px (0.375rem) for buttons and inputs.
- **Large Elements:** 10px (0.625rem) for cards and library thumbnails.
- **Special Case:** The Shutter Button is always a perfect circle (pill-shaped) to distinguish it as the primary hardware-mapped interaction point.

## Components

### Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Button:** Solid #E8500E with white text. No gradient.
- **Secondary Button:** Translucent #FFFFFF at 10% opacity with a subtle border.
- **Remote Sliders:** Ultra-thin tracks (2px) with a prominent 16px circular thumb. Use haptic feedback metaphors in the visual design (ticks for increments).

### Cards & Grid Items
- **Masonry Items:** Images should have no visible border until hovered. On hover, a 1px Signature Orange stroke appears with a metadata overlay at the bottom.
- **Metadata Overlay:** A 40% black gradient at the bottom of images with JetBrains Mono text for EXIF data.

### Controls
- **Shutter Button:** A large, circular orange button. It features a dual-ring design: a solid inner circle and a thin outer ring to mimic the "half-press" focus state of a physical Ricoh GR.
- **Segmented Controls:** Housed in a single "capsule" container with a sliding background highlight to indicate the active state.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** Semi-transparent with SF-style iconography. Active items should use a subtle grey highlight and a vertical orange indicator line (2px wide) on the far left.
